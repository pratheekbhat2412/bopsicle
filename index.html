Bopsicle Looper

This was the toughest one out of the 3 for obvious reasons. I was messing around with FL, and my MIDI cable broke. My keyboard is pretty old, and I cannot find a cheap cable to connect it. I initially recorded individual sounds (for the full length of the song/cover) and stitched them together using a basic editor. That was very time-consuming. As the saying goes, necessity is the mother of invention; I created Bopsicle Looper.

All I have to do is press record, play my keys, it loops. I can adjust the volume and trim it too. I can record 8 such tracks and overlay them to create a final song.
